# The City You Built

**Idea.** The profile is a small isometric city at dusk on the Nile in Minya. Every project is a building whose shape says what it does: the CORTEX beacon tower, the DÖSE glass flacon, the Amwag HQ with its clock and turnstiles, and the BOSLA compass observatory. Yosr is a construction site with its plans pinned to the hoarding. One isometric engine and one light model draw everything. The README reads as one walk through the city: the hero skyline (roof flags 01–09), Landmark Avenue (postcards and tiles), Market Street (a corner block of shops, one per toolbox group), Census Square (the contribution calendar as a district) and Central Station (a metro line that runs out of the cliffs, through the hall and four linked stops, and back into a tunnel). Sections are named by blue Egyptian enamel street plates painted into the art, in Arabic above English. There are no header images.

**Live data (data.json).** Hero: lit windows equal contributions in the last 30 days; there is a real moon phase and a Cairo-time clock; "on GitHub since" uses the year from user.createdAt. A third stat counts days since the last push and names the project, mapped from repo name to profile name. When a value is zero, that stat is swapped out or left out. Each postcard has a stamp printed with the city's massing model, with its own lot lit, and a postmark dated by the repo's pushedAt. "Last brick laid" also comes from pushedAt, and a building's lit share follows its recency. Market Street: a language meter by bytes, whose labels are placed only where their measured width fits. Census Square: one floor per contribution, and a floor stands for several when the busiest day passes 40. The busiest-day and today callouts avoid the board and the headline. The census board drops rows whose value is zero. If the file passes 250 KB, older towers are drawn in a simpler style.

**Phone.** Every image is a `<picture>` whose `(max-width: 600px)` source is a phone composition, `assets/m-<name>.svg`, on a 600-wide grid with the same 12-unit gutters (half tiles are 288 wide; the station pieces are the desktop strip at half scale, so the shared img widths and the unbroken line still hold). The same drawing functions are recomposed portrait: art above copy, copy at 26 units, labels at 17–21. The hero stacks the name, the city (roof flags drawn by `pinLabels` at 1.42x) and the stats as rows. Postcards re-set their detail callouts. Tiles keep number, name, kind and recency in the display face. Yosr's plan becomes a vertical timeline. Market Street adds a shop directory and a bar-and-legend meter. The census sets its board as a grid and places the busiest-day label clear of towers, ground and headline. Phone files embed per-file font subsets (`lib/subset.mjs`: woff2 decoded, unused glyphs emptied, WOFF 1.0 out, full fonts kept if anything fails), so the 17 phone files weigh about 690 KB; the phone census simplifies past 170 KB. Desktop SVGs are byte-identical to the desktop-only build: phone files are built after them, because the iso engine numbers gradients globally.

**Robustness.** Facts come only from profile.json. A new project gets a parametric townhouse: its height follows the repo's bytes and its accent follows the repo's language. A removed project leaves a pocket park in the hero. A project whose kind says "in design" gets the construction hoarding, and its doc-first steps are parsed from its line, with none drawn as done. These cases were tested with zero data, a 12x busier year, a recent busiest day, a 1,800-contributions-a-year calendar, and one project added plus one removed (phone set too). Rebuilds are byte-identical.

**Files.** `build.mjs`; `lib/` svg (doc, fonts, measuring, pills), iso (engine), buildings-a/b/c, city + hero, scene + stamp + cards + tiles, plate, toolbox + meter, activity + board, contact, readme, motion, metrics; phone: mobile (postcards and shared helpers), mobile-tiles, mobile-hero, mobile-census, mobile-market, mobile-station, mobile-all, subset. Fonts: Big Shoulders Display 800 and DM Mono 500, plus a 4 KB Reem Kufi Arabic subset fetched by `tools/get-arabic-font.mjs`. Output: `README.md` and 34 SVGs in `assets/` (17 desktop, about 1.18 MB; 17 phone, about 0.69 MB).

**Known limitations.** Each SVG keeps its own dusk background, so on the light theme the page is a column of dark cards that share one grid. On phones the station subtitles (host, handle, "résumé · pdf") are left out and live only in the alt text, and the census month names are small; the phone page is about 6,500 px tall at 380 px. The subset fonts were checked in headless Chrome only. If the embedded fonts load late, headless renders sometimes show text in a system font for a moment. The metro line runs along the isometric diagonal, which stays level on screen, so it continues across the five linked images. District names, building metaphors and the Arabic street names are design copy, not facts.
